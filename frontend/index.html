SPHEREx Blink

Watch the infrared sky move — 102 colours, every six months

  1. Pick a patch of sky. Type a name (e.g. Barnard's Star) or RA/Dec in degrees or sexagesimal, or click a test target.
  2. Press Load. The tool asks NASA/IPAC IRSA for every SPHEREx exposure covering that point, aligns them north-up and stacks each survey visit.
  3. Blink. Anything that jumps between frames is moving: nearby stars, brown dwarfs, asteroids, comets. Use Difference to cancel out everything that stays still.
  4. Check it. Turn on Gaia to see where known stars should be on each date, and read the spectrum and light curve under the viewer.

Keys: Space play/pause · ←/→ step · D cycle display · G Gaia
